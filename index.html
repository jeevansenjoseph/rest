Nuestra historia

Our Story

A neighborhood Mexican kitchen on Highway 18, feeding Apple Valley families for years.

Since day one

Food that tastes like home.

Chevo's started with a simple idea: cook the dishes we grew up eating, make them fresh, and serve them in a place where everybody feels welcome. Years later, that's still the whole plan.

Our kitchen turns out the classics the High Desert loves: carne asada, chile colorado, crispy chimichangas, hard-shell tacos, and a mariscos menu that keeps people driving across town. The chips are warm, the salsa is homemade, and the portions are generous.

Come in for breakfast, lunch or dinner. Bring the kids, bring the whole crew, or just pull up to the drive-thru.
